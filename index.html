Usability evaluation

Possible issues found while walking through the prototype, and what changed.

Problem foundImprovement made
A resident may not know which family member a certificate is for.Added a "Requesting for" selector limited to household members, repeated on the review screen.
Residents may think "Submitted" means the certificate is finished and visit too early.Confirmation screen explains the 3 next steps, and My requests says to come only when Ready.
On a slow piso net connection, a second tap on Submit could send a duplicate request.Submit disables and shows "Sending..." until the result appears.
Someone using a borrowed phone could leave their household logged in.Log out is always visible in the header on every screen.
Status shown by colour alone is hard to read in sunlight.Every status uses a word and a progress bar, not colour only.

HCI principles and where they appear

Consistency
One button style, one back/cancel position on every step.
Visibility
Emergency advisory sits at the top of Home in amber, used for nothing else.
Feedback
Inline errors, loading state, toast, and a reference number after submit.
User control
Back, Cancel with confirmation, and Change links on review.
Error prevention
Continue stays blocked until a choice is made; consent checkbox is required.
Clear navigation
Header tabs show the current page; steps show "where am I" in the request.
Visual hierarchy
One teal primary action per screen; secondary actions are outlined.
